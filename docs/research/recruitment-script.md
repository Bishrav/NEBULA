# NEBULA recruitment and participant information template

Use this neutral text only after the responsible university or institutional reviewer approves the study and consent process. Adapt contact, compensation, and withdrawal details to local requirements.

## Short recruitment invitation

We are conducting an exploratory university study of search tools for technical engineering documentation. We are looking for engineers or advanced computing students who regularly investigate architecture, deployment, or operational questions. The session takes approximately 30 minutes and involves completing three documentation-search tasks in a synthetic environment. We will study the search experience, not your expertise or job performance.

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time without penalty. The study may record anonymous session telemetry such as queries, result interactions, task outcomes, timing, and confidence ratings after consent. We will not ask you to provide company documents, passwords, names, or unrelated browsing data.

Contact: __________________

## Screening questions

Ask only the approved minimum:

1. Do you regularly search technical documentation as part of engineering or advanced computing work/study?
2. Are you at least the minimum age required by the approving institution and local policy?
3. Are you willing to use a synthetic documentation corpus and provide consent before telemetry is recorded?

Do not collect sensitive demographic or employment information unless explicitly approved and necessary for the research question.

## Participant briefing points

Before the session, explain:

- the purpose and approximate duration;
- that the participant is evaluating a tool, not being tested;
- what data is collected and what is not collected;
- that participation and telemetry consent are voluntary;
- how session codes, raw data, retention, and deletion work;
- who can access the data and whether a redacted derivative may be shared.

Use the approved consent document for the formal decision. This page is not a substitute for that document.
