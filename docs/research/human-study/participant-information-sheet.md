# Participant information sheet

**DRAFT — REQUIRES INSTITUTIONAL/SUPERVISOR REVIEW**

## Invitation

You are invited to take part in a research study about searching technical engineering documentation. Please read this information and ask any questions before deciding.

## What is the purpose?

We are comparing two ways of presenting search results for engineering questions. We want to understand whether source evidence and metadata help people find and verify a useful technical document.

## What will I do?

You will complete approximately three documentation-search tasks in a synthetic or public-safe environment. You may enter queries, open results, inspect evidence, and answer short confidence and experience questions. The session should take about 30 minutes.

## Is participation voluntary?

Yes. Participation is voluntary. You may refuse any question, stop at any time, or withdraw according to the approved withdrawal procedure. There is no penalty for stopping.

## What information will be collected?

Only the approved study data may be collected: anonymous session code, queries entered into the study environment, result interactions, task outcomes, timing, confidence responses, and optional study comments. Do not enter names, passwords, private company information, customer information, or unrelated browsing data.

The study will not intentionally collect your name, email, account identifiers, or unrelated browsing history in NEBULA telemetry. Contact and consent records, if required, will be stored separately from study session codes.

## Risks and benefits

The expected risk is low, but you may experience inconvenience, fatigue, or discomfort while completing tasks. You may stop. There may be no direct personal benefit. The study may help improve the design and evaluation of technical search systems.

## Confidentiality

The research team will restrict access to approved personnel and use the approved storage location. Published results will be aggregated or redacted. Direct quotations from free-text comments require separate review and may be omitted.
